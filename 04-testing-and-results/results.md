# Results

## Prototype Status

The repository provides a demonstration architecture and a measurement framework. Production-style performance results have not yet been established.

Do not substitute assumed or synthetic numbers for measured results.

## Results to Capture During Testing

- Number of test cases
- Correct retrieval rate
- Answer accuracy rate
- Unsupported-answer rate
- Appropriate escalation rate
- Average manual lookup time
- Average automated interaction time
- Manual hours saved at projected annual volume

## Business-Value Formula

`Annual Hours Saved = Questions per Year × Manual Minutes Saved per Question ÷ 60`

`Estimated Annual Labor Savings = Annual Hours Saved × Weighted Average Hourly Compensation`
