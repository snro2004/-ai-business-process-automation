# ROI Methodology

A real business case should separate measured facts from assumptions.

## Inputs

- Annual question volume
- Measured manual time per question
- Measured post-automation manual time
- Weighted average compensation of employees performing the work
- Implementation cost
- Recurring platform/model cost
- Support and maintenance cost
- Quantifiable error-related cost avoidance, if defensible

## Calculations

`Hours Saved = Volume × (Baseline Manual Minutes - Automated Manual Minutes) ÷ 60`

`Gross Labor Value = Hours Saved × Weighted Average Hourly Compensation`

`Net Annual Benefit = Gross Labor Value + Validated Avoided Costs - Recurring Costs`

`Simple ROI = (Annual Benefit - Annual Cost) ÷ Annual Cost × 100`

`Payback Period = Initial Investment ÷ Monthly Net Benefit`

Labor savings may represent capacity returned to the organization rather than cash reduction. That distinction should be stated explicitly.
