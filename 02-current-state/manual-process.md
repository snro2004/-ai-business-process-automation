# Current-State Manual Process

A representative manual policy-question process is:

1. Employee identifies a policy question.
2. Employee searches shared files, email, intranet pages, or asks another employee.
3. Employee opens one or more policy documents.
4. Employee scans the documents for relevant language.
5. Employee interprets the policy.
6. If the situation is unclear, the question is escalated to a policy owner or HR representative.
7. Employee receives an answer and takes the next action.

## Pain Points

- Search effort is repeated for similar questions.
- Employees may find an outdated or irrelevant document.
- Policy wording can be difficult to locate quickly.
- Answers may vary depending on who is asked.
- Exceptions still require knowledgeable human review.
