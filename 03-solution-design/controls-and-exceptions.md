# Controls and Exceptions

A production implementation should include:

- Approved-source controls for documents entering the knowledge base
- Role-based access to sensitive policies
- Document versioning and retirement procedures
- Logging of questions, retrieval results, responses, and escalations as permitted by policy
- A minimum retrieval-confidence or support standard
- Explicit refusal/escalation behavior when support is insufficient
- Human approval for sensitive decisions and exceptions
- Periodic accuracy testing after policy or model changes
- Data-retention and privacy controls

The model is an information assistant, not the owner of policy decisions.
