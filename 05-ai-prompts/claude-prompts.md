# Independent Review Prompt Library

A second model can be used as a critical reviewer rather than simply repeating the first model's work.

## Independent Process Review

Act as a skeptical senior business analyst reviewing this proposed automation. Identify misunderstood process steps, risky automation choices, missing exceptions, weak assumptions, areas requiring human review, and opportunities to simplify the design.

## Red-Team Results Review

Scrutinize the measured results. Look for denominator issues, inadequate sample size, unfair before/after comparisons, omitted effort, hidden manual steps, incorrect percentages, confirmation bias, and assumptions presented as facts. State exactly what can and cannot legitimately be claimed.

## Executive Review

Act as a CIO with five minutes to evaluate this proposal. Explain the business problem, strength of the evidence, missing information, top risks, pilot criteria, and metrics that should be monitored after implementation. Critique the proposal rather than rewriting it.
