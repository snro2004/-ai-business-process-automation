# Business Problem

Employees may need to search multiple policy documents or contact another employee to answer routine policy questions. This creates avoidable search time, repeated work, inconsistent responses, and dependence on individual knowledge.

## Opportunity

Create a searchable AI knowledge base that retrieves relevant passages from approved policy documents before generating an answer.

## Desired Business Outcome

Provide faster access to policy information while maintaining source grounding, exception handling, and human accountability.
